OOI ARRAYS AND SITES
An array is a group of platforms in one region. The 8-character SITE CODE (e.g. CE02SHSM) is
what you pass to the tools. Depth = water depth. Status "suspended/removed" means the platform
is gone but its historical data is still available. Platform-type suffixes: SM=Surface Mooring,
PM=Profiler Mooring, SP=Surface-Piercing Profiler, HYPM=Apex Profiler, SUMO=Apex Surface,
FLMA/FLMB=Flanking moorings, BP/BS=cabled Benthic/Seafloor, PS/PD=cabled Shallow/Deep Profiler,
MOAS=mobile assets (gliders/AUVs), PN=cabled Primary Node.

## Coastal Endurance Array — NE Pacific, off Oregon & Washington — ACTIVE
Eastern-boundary upwelling system; strong focus on hypoxia and ocean acidification. Two lines:
Oregon (off Newport, ~44.6N) and Washington (off Grays Harbor, ~47N), each spanning inner-shelf
(~25m), shelf (~80m), and slope (~500m). Themes: Coastal Ocean Dynamics; Climate Variability;
Ocean-Atmosphere Exchange; Turbulent Mixing.

REGIONAL CABLED ARRAY (fiber-optic, real-time, off Oregon)
============================================================
High power and bandwidth to seafloor and profiler instruments; real-time. Monitors volcanic and
hydrothermal activity, methane seeps, earthquakes, and water-column processes.

fields (ASHES, International District). Themes: Fluid-Rock Interactions; Global/Plate-Scale
