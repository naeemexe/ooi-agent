OOI INSTRUMENTS
Each entry: NAME (CLASS) — discipline | what it measures | data-product codes it produces.
The CLASS (e.g. ctdbp, phsen) is what you pass to ooi_lookup. Data-product codes cross-reference
the Data Products file. "Where" notes typical deployment when it matters.

============================================================
CORE MOORING SENSORS (on most surface / coastal moorings)
============================================================

CTD (CTDBP / CTDMO / CTDPF / CTDGV / CTDAV) — Physical
  Temperature, conductivity, salinity, pressure/depth, density. The workhorse; on nearly every
  platform. Series differ by config: CTDBP=pumped (moorings), CTDMO=inductive, CTDPF=profiler,
  CTDGV=glider, CTDAV=AUV.
  Products: TEMPWAT, CONDWAT, PRACSAL, PRESWAT, DENSITY

Dissolved Oxygen (DOSTA / DOFST) — Chemical
  Dissolved oxygen concentration. Key for hypoxia studies. DOSTA=Aanderaa optode (moorings/
  profilers); DOFST=Sea-Bird SBE43 (fast, shallow profilers).
  Products: DOCONCS (stable), DOCONCF (fast)

Seawater pH (PHSEN) — Chemical
  Seawater pH (total scale, range 7-9). Primary ocean-acidification sensor. On most coastal
  moorings, NSIF ~7 m. (Newer PHSENG/H = Sea-Bird Deep SeapHOx.)
  Products: PHTOTAL, PHWATER, Seawater_pH, PH434SI, PH578SI, ABSTHRM

Fluorometer (FLORT / FLORD / FLCDR) — Biological
  Chlorophyll-a fluorescence, optical backscatter, and CDOM. Proxy for phytoplankton biomass.
  FLORT=3-wavelength (chl + backscatter + CDOM); FLORD=2-wavelength; FLCDR=CDOM only.
  Products: CHLAFLO, FLUBSCT, CDOMFLO

Nitrate (NUTNR) — Chemical
  Dissolved nitrate by UV absorption (Sea-Bird SUNA/ISUS). Nutrient / upwelling proxy.
  Products: NITROPT

Spectrophotometer (OPTAA) — Biological
  Optical absorption and beam attenuation across wavelengths (Sea-Bird AC-S); indicates turbidity
  and particle load.
  Products: OPTABSN, OPTATTN, OPTAREF, OPTASIG, OPTCREF, OPTCSIG, OPTTEMP

Spectral Irradiance (SPKIR) — Physical
  Downwelling spectral irradiance at 7 wavelengths (light availability).
  Products: SPECTIR

Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PARAD) — Biological
  PAR (400-700 nm) available for photosynthesis vs. depth/time.
  Products: OPTPARW

Turbidity (TURBD) — Physical
  Water clarity via 700 nm backscatter (proxy for suspended particles). No standalone product.

============================================================
CARBON-SYSTEM SENSORS
============================================================

pCO2 Air-Sea (PCO2A) — Chemical
  Partial pressure of CO2 in both air and surface seawater, and the air-sea CO2 flux. On surface
  buoys. Carbon-cycle sensor.
  Products: PCO2ATM, PCO2SSW, XCO2ATM, XCO2SSW, CO2FLUX, PRESAIR

pCO2 Water (PCO2W) — Chemical
  Partial pressure of CO2 in the water column (Sunburst SAMI-CO2); ocean acidification.
  Products: PCO2WAT, CO2ABS1, CO2ABS2, CO2THRM

============================================================
CURRENTS, WAVES, AND PRESSURE
============================================================

Single Point Velocity Meter (VELPT) — Physical
  Mean 2D current velocity (speed & direction), Nortek Aquadopp. On moorings and profilers.
  Products: VELPTMN

3-D Single Point Velocity Meter (VEL3D) — Physical
  3D fluctuating (turbulent) velocity (u,v,w); small-scale mixing.
  Products: VELPTTU

Velocity Profiler / ADCP (ADCP) — Physical
  3D current-velocity profile through the water column via acoustic Doppler.
  Products: VELPROF, ECHOINT

Surface Wave Spectra (WAVSS) — Physical
  Significant wave height, period, and direction (TRIAXYS).
  Products: WAVSTAT

Seafloor Pressure (PRESF) — Physical
  Seafloor pressure — tides, storms, tsunamis.
  Products: SFLPRES

Tidal Seafloor Pressure (PREST) — Physical
  Millimeter-resolution tidal seafloor pressure (cabled: Slope Base, Axial Base, Hydrate summit).
  Products: SFLPRES

============================================================
METEOROLOGY & AIR-SEA FLUX (surface buoys)
============================================================

Bulk Meteorology (METBK) — Physical
  Full weather package + air-sea fluxes: air temp, wind, humidity, barometric pressure, short/
  longwave radiation, rain, sea-surface temp/salinity/conductivity (L1), and computed heat/
  freshwater/momentum fluxes (L2).
  Products (L1): TEMPAIR, RELHUMI, BARPRES, WINDAVG, PRECIPM, SHRTIRR, LONGIRR, CONDSRF, SALSURF,
  TEMPSRF, TEMPA2M, SPECHUM, SPHUM2M, WIND10M
  Products (L2 fluxes): HEATFLX, LATNFLX, SENSFLX, NETLIRR, NETSIRR, MOMMFLX, FRSHFLX, RAINFLX

Direct Covariance Flux (FDCHP) — Physical
  Direct air-sea flux of momentum and heat from sonic anemometer + motion sensor.
  Products: FLUXMOM, FLUXHOT, WINDTUR, TMPATUR, MOTFLUX

============================================================
BIOLOGY / IMAGING / ACOUSTICS
============================================================

Bio-acoustic Sonar (ZPLS) — Biological
  Acoustic backscatter from plankton/zooplankton. ZPLSC=coastal, ZPLSG=global.
  Products: SONBSCA

Plankton Imaging (PLIMS) — Biological
  Imaging FlowCytobot (IFCB) images phytoplankton cells (10-150 microns).
  Products: ROIPML, FLOWRT, VOLANA, LOOKTI, HUMIDI

Particulate DNA Sampler (PPSDN) — Biological
  Filters and preserves seawater for microbial DNA (cabled, Axial vents).
  Products: DNASAMP

Particle Size Analysis (PRTSZ) — Physical
  Suspended particle size & concentration (laser diffraction, Pioneer MAB). No standalone product.

Digital Still Camera (CAMDS) — Biological
  Seafloor still images for organism/feature identification.
  Products: CAMSTIL

HD Digital Video Camera (CAMHD) — Geological
  Real-time HD video of the ASHES hydrothermal vent (Axial Seamount).
  Products: HDVIDEO

============================================================
CABLED-ARRAY SEAFLOOR / VENT / GEOPHYSICS (mostly Axial Seamount & Hydrate Ridge)
============================================================

Broadband Ocean Bottom Seismometer (OBSBB) — Geological
  Earthquakes/seismicity along subduction zone and Axial volcano (real-time).
  Products: GRNDACC, GRNDVEL

Short-Period Ocean Bottom Seismometer (OBSSP) — Geological
  Small local earthquakes (0.1-100 Hz).
  Products: SGRDVEL

Bottom Pressure and Tilt (BOTPT) — Geological
  Seafloor inflation/deflation ("breathing") and tilt at Axial summit.
  Products: BOTPRES, BOTSFLU, BOTTILT

Broadband Hydrophone (HYDBB) — Physical
  Broadband underwater sound (marine life to rain).
  Products: HYDAPBB, HYDFRBB

Low-Frequency Hydrophone (HYDLF) — Physical
  Low-frequency sound (wind, rain, seismic t-phases).
  Products: HYDAPLF

Horizontal Electric Field, Pressure & Inverted Echo Sounder (HPIES) — Physical
  Currents, bottom pressure, and acoustic travel time for water-column structure.
  Products: IES_HEF, IES_WPP, IESAHWV, IESHEAT, IESPRES, IESRATT

Hydrothermal Vent Fluid In-situ Chemistry (THSPH) — Chemical
  Vent-fluid temperature, hydrogen, hydrogen sulfide, pH.
  Products: THSPHTE, THSPHHC, THSPHHS, THSPHPH

Hydrothermal Vent Fluid Temperature & Resistivity (TRHPH) — Geological
  Vent temperature, resistivity (chlorinity), and redox (Eh).
  Products: TRHPHTE, TRHPHCC, TRHPHEH, TRHPHR1, TRHPHR2, TRHPHR3, TRHPHVC, TRHPHVO, TRHPHVS

Hydrothermal Vent Fluid Interactive Sampler (RASFL) — Chemical
  Collects/preserves vent-fluid samples; measures vent temperature.
  Products: TEMPVNT

Diffuse Vent Fluid 3-D Temperature Array (TMPSF) — Physical
  24-point 3D temperature field of diffuse hydrothermal flow (ASHES).
  Products: TEMPSFL

Mass Spectrometer (MASSP) — Chemical
  In-situ dissolved gases (CO2, CH4, H2S, etc.) at vents/seeps.
  Products: MASSPEC

Benthic Fluid Flow (FLOBN) — Chemical
  Fluid flow at the sediment-water interface at methane seeps (Hydrate Ridge).
  Products: BENTHFL

Osmosis-Based Water Sampler (OSMOI) — Chemical
  Long-term sampling of diffuse/pore fluids (vents and seeps).
  Products: PHSSAMP

============================================================
ENGINEERING
============================================================

Engineering (ENG / MOPAK / etc.) — Engineering
  Housing temp/humidity, motion (MOPAK 3-axis motion pack), ground-fault detection, controllers.
  Used for calibration and QC, not primary science. No science data products.
